About Me

Hi! I’m Jaxon Martarano, a game developer and interactive media creator based in Boise, Idaho. I’ve loved games for as long as I can remember, especially Nintendo games, and that eventually turned into wanting to understand how those experiences are actually designed and built.

I’m currently studying GIMM (Games, Interactive Media, and Mobile Technology) at Boise State University. I’ve worked on projects ranging from interactive comics and AR games to multiplayer systems, Roblox experiences, and my current Unreal Engine city-builder, Crisis City.

I especially enjoy game design, systems design, project leadership, and bringing all the different pieces of a project together. I like experimenting with ideas, figuring out what makes a game fun, and working with other developers and artists to turn those ideas into something playable.

Crisis City

Unreal Engine 5 C++ Game Design In Development
School Project • 2026 • Currently in Development

Game Overview

Crisis City is a city-building game I’m currently developing in Unreal Engine 5. The goal is to let players build and manage their own city while constantly having to respond to problems, make decisions, and deal with how those choices affect the city over time.

I wanted the game to be more than just placing buildings and watching numbers go up. As the city grows, players have to balance things like money, population, pollution, approval, and trust while reacting to different crises and events. The choices you make shape how the city develops and can eventually lead to different outcomes at the end of your term.

The project is still pretty early in development, so the screenshot shown here is very much a work in progress. I’m currently focused on getting the major gameplay systems working before moving further into the city’s visuals, residents, vehicles, UI, and overall atmosphere.

Current Features

  • Built a road and building placement system with straight and curved roads, snapping, intersections, placement previews, and construction costs.
  • Developed interconnected systems for population, housing, businesses, pollution, money, approval, and trust.
  • Created a crisis and decision system where choices can have immediate effects, delayed consequences, and long-term impacts on the city.
  • Designed a four-year city term with multiple possible endings based on how the player manages and develops the city.
  • Added a first-person mode that lets players leave the management view and explore the city they built.

Vyre’s Veil

Project Lead Artist Animator
School Group Project

Project Overview

Vyre’s Veil is an interactive comic exploring ideas around the bidirectional flow of time, the block universe, and externalism. Players follow Vyre, a third-dimensional being trapped in the fourth dimension who gains the ability to look forward and backward through time.

I served as the project leader, artist, and animator. I helped guide the project from its original concept through production while also developing the visual style and creating much of the artwork and animation.

Project Details

  • Led the team’s production planning, scope, and milestones throughout development.
  • Developed the project’s visual style and created artwork and animations used throughout the experience.
  • Helped design how the story and time-based ideas would be communicated through interaction rather than only dialogue.

Programmer Level Designer AR

Game Overview

Card Casters is a 2D platformer with augmented reality elements. Players control a wizard trying to recover his stolen hat while using physical magic cards to unlock abilities and create platforms inside the game.

I was one of three programmers and the team’s level designer. I worked on core gameplay mechanics while also designing the levels around the abilities players gained from the AR card system.
